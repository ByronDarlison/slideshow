Cash Forecasting · EO Accelerator

A red critical number triggers a Situation Report.

Situation

Average accounts receivable days reached 42 against our target of 35. Customer payments are arriving later than planned.

Cause

We follow up only after invoices become overdue. Missing purchase orders and incorrect billing contacts delay payment.

Correction

The collections owner will check invoice receipt and payment requirements within one week of billing. They will resolve outstanding blockers immediately.

Follow-up

The collections owner will report progress each Thursday. At month-end, we will check whether average AR days returned to 35 or below.

Situation Report guideSituation · Cause · Correction · Follow-up

Target time: 2 minutes and 30 seconds.

The function owner writes one report when the critical number turns red.

Situation: Average accounts receivable days reached 42 against our target of 35. Customer payments are arriving later than planned.

Cause: We follow up only after invoices become overdue. Missing purchase orders and incorrect billing contacts delay payment.

Correction: The collections owner checks invoice receipt and payment requirements within one week of billing. They resolve outstanding blockers immediately.

Follow-up: The collections owner reports progress each Thursday. At month-end, confirm whether average accounts receivable days returned to 35 or below.

Situation states the factual gap. Cause identifies the structure behind that gap.

Correction changes the process. Follow-up defines a date, metric, and check-in. Thursday progress checks continue before the month-end confirmation; corrective action does not wait.

Do not write another report while awaiting the agreed follow-up date.

At that date, close a corrected situation. A remaining red requires a new report and correction.

The method draws on The Managerial Moment of Truth by Bruce Bodaken and Robert Fritz.

Byron's Scoreboard Day article describes its use in this weekly cadence.

This cause and correction are illustrative, not a verified account of the historical 42-day result.

The cash consequence may not appear yet.

Weekly review manages the operating cause.

It does not update the rolling forecast.

Transition: Action corrects performance. Follow-up confirms whether the driver has returned to target.
